隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新日期：2026年6月8日 / Last updated: June 8, 2026

本隐私政策适用于 Website 应用及相关服务（以下简称“本应用”）。我们重视用户隐私，并尽量减少对个人信息的收集和使用。

This Privacy Policy applies to the Website app and related services (the "App"). We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the collection and use of personal information.

请在提交 App Store 审核前确认：本页面内容需要与 App 的实际数据收集、权限使用、第三方 SDK 和 App Store Connect 隐私标签保持一致。
Before submitting the App for App Store review, please make sure this page matches the App's actual data collection, permission usage, third-party SDKs, and App Store Connect privacy labels.

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

在当前版本中，本应用不要求用户注册账号，也不主动收集可直接识别您身份的个人信息。

In the current version, the App does not require user registration and does not actively collect personal information that directly identifies you.

  • 当您主动通过电子邮件、GitHub Issue 或其他渠道联系我们时，我们可能会收到您提供的联系方式、反馈内容和相关技术信息。
    If you contact us by email, GitHub Issues, or another channel, we may receive the contact details, feedback, and technical information you choose to provide.
  • 如果设备系统、App Store 或第三方平台提供崩溃日志、诊断数据、下载统计等信息，这些信息可能由相应平台按照其隐私政策处理。
    If the device system, App Store, or third-party platforms provide crash logs, diagnostic data, download statistics, or similar information, that information may be processed by those platforms according to their own privacy policies.
  • 我们不会出售用户个人信息，也不会将用户个人信息用于跨 App 或跨网站追踪。
    We do not sell users' personal information and do not use personal information to track users across apps or websites.

2. 权限与设备数据 / Permissions and Device Data

本应用仅在实现功能所必需时请求系统权限。若未来版本需要访问相机、照片、位置、通知、麦克风、通讯录或其他敏感权限，我们会在系统权限弹窗或应用界面中说明用途，并在获得授权后使用。

The App requests system permissions only when they are necessary for app functionality. If a future version needs access to the camera, photos, location, notifications, microphone, contacts, or other sensitive permissions, we will explain the purpose in the system permission prompt or within the App and use the permission only after authorization.

3. 信息使用方式 / How We Use Information

我们可能将您主动提供的信息用于以下目的：

We may use information you voluntarily provide for the following purposes:

  • 回复您的问题、反馈或支持请求；
    To respond to your questions, feedback, or support requests.
  • 排查错误、改进应用体验和维护服务安全；
    To troubleshoot issues, improve the app experience, and maintain service security.
  • 履行法律法规或监管要求。
    To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or lawful requests from authorities.

4. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

本应用当前不包含广告跟踪 SDK。若未来集成统计分析、云服务、支付、广告或其他第三方服务，我们会在本隐私政策中说明相关服务的名称、用途和数据处理方式，并同步更新 App Store Connect 中的隐私信息。

The App currently does not include advertising tracking SDKs. If future versions integrate analytics, cloud services, payments, advertising, or other third-party services, we will describe the service names, purposes, and data processing practices in this Privacy Policy and update the privacy information in App Store Connect accordingly.

5. 信息共享 / Information Sharing

除以下情况外，我们不会向第三方共享您的个人信息：

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following situations:

  • 获得您的明确同意；
    With your explicit consent.
  • 为提供必要服务而与受托服务提供方共享，且其只能按照我们的指示处理信息；
    With service providers when necessary to provide services, provided they process information only according to our instructions.
  • 为遵守适用法律法规、法院命令或政府机关的合法要求。
    To comply with applicable laws, court orders, or lawful government requests.

6. 数据保留与删除 / Data Retention and Deletion

我们仅在实现本政策所述目的所需期间保留您主动提供的信息。您可以联系我们请求访问、更正或删除相关信息；在法律允许的范围内，我们会在合理时间内处理您的请求。

We retain information you voluntarily provide only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of relevant information, and we will process your request within a reasonable time where permitted by law.

7. 儿童隐私 / Children's Privacy

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如果您认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会尽快采取合理措施删除相关信息。

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it as soon as possible.

8. 信息安全 / Information Security

我们会采取合理的技术和管理措施保护信息安全。但请理解，任何互联网传输或电子存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect information security. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. 政策更新 / Policy Updates

我们可能会根据应用功能、法律法规或运营需要更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策会发布在本页面，并更新“最后更新日期”。重大变更将尽可能通过应用内提示或其他合理方式通知您。

We may update this Privacy Policy based on app functionality, legal requirements, or operational needs. Updated policies will be posted on this page with the "Last updated" date revised. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you through in-app notices or other appropriate means.

10. 联系我们 / Contact Us

如果您对本隐私政策或个人信息处理有任何问题，请通过 GitHub 仓库联系我们： https://github.com/kingla0624/website/issues

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of personal information, please contact us through the GitHub repository: https://github.com/kingla0624/website/issues
